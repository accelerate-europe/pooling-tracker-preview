Method

What is listed

AI training runs whose sites are geographically apart, whether the run was real, emulated or simulated, and deployments built to train across such sites.

Runs at one site are left out, as are runs inside one company’s own data-centre estate across buildings of one cluster or at sites whose separation is not disclosed.

A run is listed only when a source gives at least one of its size (parameters or tokens), its hardware (number or type of accelerators) or its interconnect (bandwidth, latency or distance between sites).

Coordination

  • Single coordinator. One organisation runs the training across several of its own sites.
  • Multiple coordinators. Several organisations contribute compute to one run.
  • Federated. Participants train where their data sits and share model updates rather than data.

Evidence

Runs fall into two groups. Demonstrated runs are drawn with solid links, indicative runs with dashed ones.

  • Demonstrated. A real training run across the sites described.
  • Indicative. Evidence that points to pooled training without showing a real run across the sites. It covers five grades:
  • Simulated. Modelled rather than run.
  • Emulated. Run on one cluster with the conditions between sites imposed, such as limited bandwidth or added latency.
  • Reported. A third party reports the run and the organisation has not confirmed it. A report enters the record only when at least two independent established outlets carry it, or one does with checkable detail such as named sites, documents or a named source, and the organisation has not denied it.
  • Claim. Stated by an organisation without a documented run.
  • Deployment. Infrastructure built to train across sites, without published figures from a run.

Figures

Every figure comes from a source that was opened and read. A field stays empty when the source does not give it. Figures are never estimated, and never derived from other figures; a change of unit is stated in the note.

Figures an organisation reports about its own run are marked self-reported. Sources are graded Primary, Secondary or Informal. Efficiency figures mix compute utilisation, throughput ratios and vendor claims, so the note under each says which it is.

Places

A site goes on the map only when a source names the place. A site named only by country or state sits at the region’s capital as a nominal point and is drawn as a hollow ring .

Record

Sources are scanned weekly and each addition is reviewed before it enters the record. Each run has its own address, so it can be linked and cited.
